# Changelog

## 1.0.0

### Added
- Three new nether fortresses: intact, damaged and ruined. The more intact the fortress, the tougher the fights and the better the loot
- Corridors, crossings, staircases and an upper level of open walkways, plus a tower in the intact fortress
- Libraries, TNT rooms and treasure chests, with loot matched to each fortress
- Common chests use the vanilla nether fortress loot, so loot added by other mods still shows up
- Each fortress has its own crews, fewer and weaker the more ruined the fortress
- Blaze, skeleton, wither skeleton, magma cube and piglin brute spawners
- Basalt columns and basalt delta lava pools don't generate inside the fortresses
- Replaces the vanilla nether fortress by default, and Moog's Nether Structures' mega fortress when that mod is installed. This can be turned off in Moog's Structure Lib's config
